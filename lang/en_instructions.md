
## General

Enter your start date. If needed, switch to advanced mode to set a different current date.

For **Remaining activity (months)**, enter the value currently shown in your prolongation template.

The calculator automatically accounts for today and adds any missing activity up to the next cutoff date, either 30 June or 31 December.

Check the **Milestones** and **Statistics** panels below the plot for the calculated output.

## Advanced

Choose between **Timeline view** and **Activity view** depending on whether you want to see milestone dates on a timeline or as activity percentage milestones.

Enable **comparison mode** in advanced settings if you want to compare two prolongation scenarios.

## Tabs

Use the **Average prolongation** tab to estimate milestones based on one overall future prolongation percentage.

Use the **Semester prolongation** tab if you want to enter different prolongation values for each semester.

Open the **Changelog** tab to see what has changed in the app over time.
